Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

AC Intelligence keeps this short because we collect very little. This page covers two things: what happens to anything you send through this website, and how our internal tools use Google sign-in.

This website

The site uses no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to run and protect the site.

If you fill in the audit request form, your name, company, email and message go to our inbox at consult@acintelligence.net through FormSubmit, a form delivery service. We use them only to reply to you. We don't sell them, share them or add you to a mailing list.

Our tools and Google sign-in

We run internal tools, including one called Workshop Assistant, that sign in with a Google account. With the account owner's permission, they read and update that account's Google Sheets, Forms, Drive files and Apps Script projects to automate work such as reports, form responses and team notifications.

  • Google data is used only to do the task the account owner asked for.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. We don't copy Google data to our own servers.
  • We don't sell Google data, share it with third parties, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can remove a tool's access at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Deleting your information

Email us and we'll delete what we hold about you, including past form submissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to consult@acintelligence.net. If we change it, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
